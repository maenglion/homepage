Overview

Soul Spectrum operates as an independent research laboratory. We design, build, and validate systems that measure the implicit signals in human communication. Our work bridges cognitive science, acoustic engineering, and data architecture.

We prioritize methodological rigor and reproducible protocols over rapid commercial scaling. Our core engines, CASP and SLDA, are built on Bayesian principles and designed to operate with high transparency and strict data privacy.
