CASP Engine

A real-time processing pipeline for extracting and indexing paralinguistic features.

System Architecture

The CASP Engine is designed to process audio streams in real time, bypassing traditional STT bottlenecks. It extracts low-level acoustic features and maps them into a high-dimensional variance index.

  • Audio Ingestion: Real-time chunking of audio streams.
  • Feature Extraction: Computation of Mel-frequency cepstral coefficients (MFCCs), pitch, jitter, shimmer, and energy envelopes.
  • Baseline Calibration: Dynamic anchor-point generation based on initial neutral segments.
  • Variance Scoring: Continuous calculation of deviation from the baseline, outputting a multidimensional stress/load index.

Privacy by Design

Because the engine analyzes acoustic features rather than semantic content, it is inherently more privacy-preserving than STT-based systems. The primary feature extraction (labeling and anonymization) can be performed on the edge device, ensuring that raw voice data does not need to be transmitted to central servers.
