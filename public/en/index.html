CASP  Cognitive–Adaptive Stress Paralinguistics

Not what is said,
but how it is spoken

CASP ENGINE extracts non-verbal acoustic features—speech rate, pauses, rhythm, jitter, and energy variance—without relying on Speech-to-Text (STT). By establishing a personalized baseline, it measures deviations that indicate cognitive load, stress, or emotional shifts in real time.

SLDA  Semantic Latent Dirichlet Allocation

Mapping semantic drift
in texts and arguments

While CASP measures speech, SLDA analyzes text. It tracks how the meaning of specific terms or the structure of an argument drifts over time or across different contexts. It maps the latent semantic shifts in disputes, documents, and discourse.
