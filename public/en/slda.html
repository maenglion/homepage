Methodology

SLDA

Semantic Latent Dirichlet Allocation.
Mapping the semantic and logical drift in texts, disputes, and discourse.

Tracking Semantic Drift

SLDA is an analytical framework designed to measure how the meaning of terms, the structure of arguments, and the underlying logic shift over time. By applying topic modeling and semantic collision mapping (SCM), SLDA visualizes the latent structure of complex documents and disputes.

This methodology is particularly useful in analyzing policy documents, legal arguments, and extended public discourse, where the definition of key terms often drifts subtly to serve different logical endpoints.

Data Privacy and Ethical Compliance

The SLDA analysis pipeline strictly adheres to the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and current AI ethics standards regarding data minimization and purpose limitation.

To protect sensitive information, the most critical stages of data processing—primary transformation, labeling, and anonymization—are executed locally on the user's device. The original raw text is never transmitted to our servers. Only the anonymized structural vectors are uploaded for final computation.

English Service Status

The automated SLDA analysis service is currently optimized for Korean semantics. The English-language service is under development. Therefore, direct application for analysis is not available on the English site at this time.
