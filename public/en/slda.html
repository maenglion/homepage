Mapping the semantic and logical drift in texts, disputes, and discourse.

What SLDA Measures

SLDA (Semantic Logic Drift Analysis) is an original analytical framework developed at Soul Spectrum. It measures, at the sentence level, four structural dimensions of logical and semantic change in documents and disputes:

  • Argument drift (논점 이동): Whether the central claim shifts across the document without explicit acknowledgment.
  • Expressive intensity shift (표현강도 변화): Whether the rhetorical force applied to a given claim increases or decreases over time.
  • Subject attribution transition (주체귀속 전환): Whether the responsible agent assigned to an action or claim changes across statements.
  • Premise conflict (전제충돌): Whether later statements contradict the logical premises established earlier in the same document.

SLDA does not judge the truth or merit of a claim. It measures the quantity, distribution, and amplitude of structural change—and leaves interpretation to the reader.

Data Privacy and Ethical Compliance

The SLDA analysis pipeline strictly adheres to the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and APEC CBPR frameworks. We ensure compliance through a strict Privacy by Design architecture:

  • Data Minimisation: We collect only the structural metadata (labels and tags) required for analysis.
  • Local Preprocessing: The primary transformation of raw text—including masking, labeling, and anonymization of names, identifiers, and sensitive information—is executed entirely on the user's local device.
  • Storage & Purpose Limitation: Original raw text is never transmitted to our servers. The anonymized data received is used exclusively for statistical and drift analysis before being securely discarded.

This architecture is specifically designed to eliminate legal risks and meet the highest standards of security and anonymization required for processing sensitive data in medical, legal, and institutional contexts.
